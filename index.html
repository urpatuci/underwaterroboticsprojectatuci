About Us

Welcome to Underwater Robotics UCI, a student-run engineering organization at the University of California, specializing in autonomous underwater robotics and competing in the annual 2026 RoboSub competition this summer. Our team is passionate about pushing the boundaries of technology and innovation in the underwater world. Join us as we dive into new challenges and explore the depths of engineering!

Our Mission

Based at the University of California Irvine, our group is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of engineering and technology in the underwater world. Our members come from a diverse range of backgrounds, including all types of engineering and computer science. Together, we work towards designing and building advanced robotic submarines that can navigate through challenging underwater obstacles and complete complex tasks.
